## Source and derived data

The analytical source is the Our World in Data Energy dataset, whose codebook
documents provider-level lineage, including Ember's Yearly Electricity Data and
the Energy Institute Statistical Review of World Energy. Source and derived data
retain the attribution and reuse conditions of their upstream providers. Users
should consult the current [OWID data-reuse guidance](https://ourworldindata.org/how-to-use-our-world-in-data)
and the provider lineage in `owid-energy-codebook.csv` before redistribution.

The repository's derived panel is supplied solely to make the published analysis
auditable and reproducible. No ownership claim is made over third-party source
measurements.
